  • Guar (Cyamopsis tetragonoloba), or cluster bean, is a vital drought-tolerant legume. It is processed to extract Guar Gum, an industrial emulsifier used in food, cosmetics, and shale gas extraction.

  • Guar is an arid crop thriving in high heat (25°C–38°C) and dry sandy soils.

  • Guar needs low water (200–400 mm). Waterlogging damages the crop severely.

  • Sown in July (Kharif) or March–April (Zaid), it matures in 90–120 days.

  • India produces over 80% of global guar gum, with Rajasthan being the dominant producer.
