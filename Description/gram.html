  • Gram (Cicer arietinum), also called chickpea or Chana, is the single largest pulse crop produced in India. Accounts for over 40% of India's total pulse basket, consumed as chana dal, besan (gram flour), or whole roasted gram.

  • Gram is a cool climate Rabi crop grown in winter. The optimum temperature range is 15°C to 25°C. Severe frost during flowering causes pod abortion.

  • Gram is a deep-rooted, drought-tolerant crop requiring low water (250–350 mm). Excess soil moisture or heavy rains cause root rot and wilt diseases.

  • Sowing occurs from October to November. The crop matures in 90–120 days (Desi varieties) to 110–140 days (Kabuli varieties), harvested in March–April.

  • Gram contains 20-22% high-grade protein, fiber, and iron. India produces over 70% of global chickpea supply with Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Karnataka leading production.
