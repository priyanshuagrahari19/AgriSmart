  • Pumpkin (Cucurbita moschata), or Kaddu, is a warm-season cucurbit vine vegetable grown widely across India.

  • Pumpkin grows best in warm conditions (20°C–32°C). Frost damages plants severely.

  • Pumpkin needs moderate watering (400–500 mm). Well-drained sandy loams are ideal.

  • Pumpkin is cultivated as a Zaid (Feb–March sowing) and Kharif crop. Matures in 90–120 days.

  • Rich in Vitamin A (beta-carotene), Vitamin C, and antioxidants. Major producing states include Uttar Pradesh, Orissa, Assam, and Bihar.
