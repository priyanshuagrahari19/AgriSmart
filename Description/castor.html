  • Castor (Ricinus communis), known as Arandi, is a non-edible commercial oilseed crop valued for castor oil used in lubricants, paints, pharmaceuticals, and biodiesel.

  • Castor grows best in warm tropical climates (20°C–35°C). Frost damages plants severely.

  • Castor needs moderate water (500–700 mm). Deep well-drained loamy soils support deep tap root systems.

  • Sown in July–August or early summer, castor is a long-duration crop (150–180 days) with multiple harvesting pickings.

  • India produces over 85% of global castor oil, led by Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Andhra Pradesh.
