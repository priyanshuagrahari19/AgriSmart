  • Masoor (Lens culinaris), commonly known as red lentil, is a key pulse crop in Rabi season. Consumed as split red dal or whole grain, it is one of the oldest human cultivated legumes.

  • Masoor requires cool climatic conditions during growth (18°C–25°C) and warm weather during pod maturation. Severe frost or excess humidity increases disease susceptibility.

  • Masoor is a low water crop (200–300 mm), making it well-suited for rainfed conditions and residual moisture cultivation in rice fallows.

  • Masoor is sown in October–November and harvested in March–April. It matures in 110–130 days.

  • Masoor is rich in protein (26%), folate, iron, and potassium. Major producing states include Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and West Bengal.
