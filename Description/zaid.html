Zaid Crops

Zaid crops are summer crops grown in India during the short warm period between the Rabi (winter) and Kharif (monsoon) seasons, mainly from March to June. These crops mature quickly and require warm dry weather for growth and long day-length for flowering. Major Zaid crops include watermelons, cucumbers, muskmelons, bitter gourd, pumpkin, cowpea, ragi, and fodder crops.


Zaid crops provide vital income to farmers during the dry summer months when main fields are empty between Rabi harvesting and Kharif sowing. High in water and nutrient content, Zaid crops are essential for summer hydration and food security across India.
