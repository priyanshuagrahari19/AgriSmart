  • Oats (Avena sativa), known as Jai, is a nutritious cereal and green fodder crop. Cultivated for human cereal (oatmeal, rolled oats) and livestock feed, it belongs to the grass family Poaceae.

  • Oats thrive in cool temperate conditions (12°C–22°C). They are more tolerant of wet, cool weather and acid soils than wheat or barley.

  • Oats require moderate water (350–500 mm). Well-drained loam to clay loam soils support sturdy stem growth and fodder biomass.

  • Oats are sown in October–November as a Rabi crop. Green fodder is cut in 50–60 days, while grain crop matures in 120–135 days.

  • Oats contain beta-glucan soluble fiber, which reduces blood cholesterol. Highly popular as health breakfast foods, major growing regions include Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh.
