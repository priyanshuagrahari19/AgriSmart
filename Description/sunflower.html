  • Sunflower (Helianthus annuus), known as Surajmukhi, is a commercial oilseed crop valued for its light, healthy edible oil. Its yellow flower heads exhibit heliotropism (following the sun).

  • Sunflower is day-length neutral and can be grown in any season. Ideal growth temperature is 20°C–28°C with full bright sunlight.

  • Sunflower requires moderate water (500–600 mm). Bud initiation, flowering, and seed filling are critical irrigation stages.

  • Though grown year-round, Rabi sunflower (sown Nov–Dec) yields high quality oil seeds harvested in March–April. Crop matures in 90–100 days.

  • Sunflower oil is rich in polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA) and Vitamin E. Karnataka is the leading sunflower producer in India, followed by Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Bihar.
