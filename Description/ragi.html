  • Ragi (Eleusine coracana), or finger millet, is a superfood nutri-cereal cultivated extensively in drylands. It is famous for its high calcium, fiber, and iron contents.

  • Ragi thrives in warm tropical climates (22°C–32°C). It tolerates drought well and survives in poor, acidic, or marginal soils.

  • Ragi requires low water (350–450 mm) and can be grown easily in rainfed drylands.

  • Ragi is cultivated during Kharif and Zaid seasons. It matures in 100–120 days.

  • Ragi has 5–30 times more calcium than wheat or rice (344 mg / 100g). Karnataka produces over 55% of India's total ragi output.
