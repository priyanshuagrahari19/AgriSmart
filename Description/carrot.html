  • Carrot (Daucus carota) is a root vegetable cultivated across temperate and tropical regions. Celebrated for its bright orange or deep red color, it belongs to the Apiaceae family and is consumed raw in salads, cooked, or processed into juices and halwa.

  • Carrot is a cool-season crop requiring temperatures between 15°C and 20°C for deep root color development. Temperatures above 25°C reduce root elongation and alter flavor.

  • Carrots need uniform soil moisture. Deep, loose, friable sandy loam soils free of stones allow smooth, straight root growth without branching.

  • In India, Asiatic types are sown in August–September, while European varieties are sown in October–November (Rabi season). Harvest takes place 80–100 days after sowing.

  • Carrots are an exceptional source of beta-carotene (Vitamin A precursor), fiber, potassium, and antioxidants. Major producing states in India include Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu.
