  • Cowpea (Vigna unguiculata), known as Lobia or Barbati, is an important legume cultivated for green pods, dry seeds, and fodder.

  • Cowpea is a warm-weather crop requiring temperatures between 20°C and 35°C. It is drought tolerant.

  • Cowpea needs low to moderate water (300–450 mm). Well-drained soils are essential.

  • Cultivated in Kharif and Zaid seasons. Green pods are harvested in 50–65 days.

  • Rich in protein (24%), soluble fiber, and iron. Enhances soil nitrogen fertility. Grown widely in Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, and Rajasthan.
