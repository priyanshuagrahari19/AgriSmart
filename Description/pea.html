  • Pea (Pisum sativum), commonly known as Matar, is an important cool-season pulse and vegetable crop grown worldwide. Consumed fresh, frozen, or as dried split pulse, it is a key component of winter diets.

  • Peas thrive in cool weather with ideal temperatures ranging between 10°C and 22°C. Frost during flowering causes flower and pod damage, while hot dry weather causes early pod ripening and low yield.

  • Peas require light to moderate irrigation (350–500 mm). Flowering and early pod filling stages are critical irrigation periods. Well-drained fertile loamy soils with neutral pH ensure healthy crop growth.

  • In India, pea is grown as a major Rabi crop sown in October–November and harvested between January and March. Early varieties mature in 60–70 days for green pods.

  • Peas are packed with plant protein (7%), dietary fiber, Vitamin C, Vitamin K, and lutein. Economically, green peas fetch high market rates, while dried field peas contribute to pulse production. Leading states include Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Punjab.
